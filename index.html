Warrior Guide

Level 1-60

1. Quick Facts

Best Race (PvE): Human / Orc
Best Race (PvP): Gnome / Orc
Leveling Spec: Arms
Difficulty: Hard (Gear Dependent)

2. Talent Tree & Leveling Order

  • Level 10–19: Cruelty (5/5), Deflection (4/5)
    Focus on getting flat crit chance first for consistent rage generation.
  • Level 20–29: Tactical Mastery (5/5), Improved Overpower (2/2), Deep Wounds (3/3)
    Tactical Mastery is mandatory for smooth stance dancing.
  • Level 30–39: Impale (2/2), Sweeping Strikes (1/1), Weapon Mastery (2/2), Mortal Strike (1/1)
    Sweeping Strikes and Mortal Strike are your biggest leveling power spikes.

3. Rotation & Playstyle

Single Target

Charge → Rend → Mortal Strike → Overpower (on proc). Use Heroic Strike only if rage > 60.

Multi-Target

Sweeping Strikes, switch to Berserker Stance, Whirlwind, Cleave. Hamstring kite to survive.

4. Stat Priority

Weapon DPS > Strength > Agility > Stamina

Paladin Guide

Level 1-60

1. Quick Facts

Best Race (PvE): Human / Undead
Best Race (PvP): Dwarf / Tauren
Leveling Spec: Retribution
Difficulty: Easy

2. Talent Tree & Leveling Order

  • Level 10–19: Divine Strength (5/5), Improved Seal of the Crusader (4/5)
  • Level 20–29: Seal of Command (1/1), Pursuit of Justice (2/2), Conviction (5/5)
  • Level 30–39: Sanctity Aura (1/1), Two-Handed Weapon Spec (3/3), Vengeance (5/5)

3. Rotation & Playstyle

Single Target

Keep Blessing of Might active. Seal of the Crusader → Judgement → Seal of Command → Auto Attack.

Multi-Target

Retribution Aura to reflect damage. Hammer of Justice to heal safely. Divine Shield for emergencies.

4. Stat Priority

Weapon DPS > Strength > Stamina > Intellect

Hunter Guide

Level 1-60

1. Quick Facts

Best Race (PvE): Troll / Night Elf
Best Race (PvP): Orc / Dwarf
Leveling Spec: Beast Mastery
Difficulty: Very Easy

2. Talent Tree & Leveling Order

  • Level 10–19: Endurance Training (5/5), Thick Hide (3/3), Improved Revive Pet (2/2)
    Makes your pet an unkillable tank while leveling.
  • Level 20–29: Pathfinding (2/2), Bestial Swiftness (1/1), Unleashed Fury (5/5), Ferocity (2/5)
  • Level 30–39: Ferocity (5/5), Intimidation (1/1), Frenzy (5/5), Bestial Wrath (1/1)
    Bestial Wrath turns your pet into a literal god every 2 minutes.

3. Rotation & Playstyle

Single Target

Hunter's Mark → Send Pet → Serpent Sting → Auto Shot. Only use Multi-Shot/Arcane Shot if you have excess mana. Let your pet hold aggro.

Multi-Target

Set pet on one target, trap a second with Freezing Trap, and kite/shoot a third. Feign Death if you pull aggro from your pet.

4. Stat Priority

Ranged Weapon DPS > Agility > Intellect > Stamina

Rogue Guide

Level 1-60

1. Quick Facts

Best Race (PvE): Human / Orc
Best Race (PvP): Dwarf / Undead
Leveling Spec: Combat Swords
Difficulty: Medium

2. Talent Tree & Leveling Order

  • Level 10–19: Improved Sinister Strike (2/2), Improved Gouge (3/3), Deflection (4/5)
  • Level 20–29: Deflection (5/5), Riposte (1/1), Precision (5/5), Endurance (1/2)
  • Level 30–39: Dual Wield Spec (4/5), Blade Flurry (1/1), Sword Spec (5/5)
    Sword Spec and Blade Flurry turn you into a leveling machine.

3. Rotation & Playstyle

Single Target

Stealth → Garrote/Cheap Shot → Sinister Strike to 1-2 combo points → Slice and Dice. Maintain SnD at all times. Eviscerate at 5 combo points if the mob will survive it.

Multi-Target & Survival

Pop Blade Flurry and Evasion to fight 2-3 mobs at once. If things go bad, use Gouge, run away, and cast Sprint or Vanish.

4. Stat Priority

Weapon DPS (Main Hand) > Agility > Strength > Stamina

Priest Guide

Level 1-60

1. Quick Facts

Best Race (PvE): Dwarf / Troll
Best Race (PvP): Dwarf / Undead
Leveling Spec: Shadow (Wand Spec)
Difficulty: Easy (Low Downtime)

2. Talent Tree & Leveling Order

  • Level 10–19: Spirit Tap (5/5), Wand Specialization (5/5)
    This combination gives you zero mana issues. Wands deal massive damage early on.
  • Level 20–29: Shadow Focus (5/5), Mind Flay (1/1), Improved Shadow Word: Pain (2/2), Shadow Weaving (2/5)
  • Level 30–39: Shadow Weaving (5/5), Vampiric Embrace (1/1), Darkness (5/5), Shadowform (1/1)

3. Rotation & Playstyle

Single Target

Power Word: Shield → Mind Blast → Shadow Word: Pain → Auto-attack with Wand until dead. Let Spirit Tap regen your mana while wanding.

Multi-Target

Dot multiple enemies with Shadow Word: Pain, use Psychic Scream to fear them away, and nuke them down one by one. Heal with Renew.

4. Stat Priority

Wand DPS > Spirit > Intellect > Stamina

Shaman Guide

Level 1-60

1. Quick Facts

Best Race (PvE): Orc / Troll
Best Race (PvP): Orc / Tauren
Leveling Spec: Enhancement
Difficulty: Medium

2. Talent Tree & Leveling Order

  • Level 10–19: Shield Spec (5/5), Thundering Strikes (5/5)
  • Level 20–29: Two-Handed Axes/Maces (1/1), Flurry (5/5), Anticipation (4/5)
  • Level 30–39: Windfury Weapon, Stormstrike (1/1), Elemental Weapons (3/3)

3. Rotation & Playstyle

Single Target

Keep Rockbiter (1-29) or Windfury (30+) active on weapon. Drop Searing Totem. Earth Shock on cooldown. Auto attack.

Multi-Target

Stoneclaw Totem to taunt off you. Magma Totem for AoE. Healing Stream Totem to stay alive.

4. Stat Priority

Weapon DPS > Strength > Agility > Stamina

Mage Guide

Level 1-60

1. Quick Facts

Best Race (PvE): Gnome / Troll
Best Race (PvP): Gnome / Undead
Leveling Spec: Frost (AoE or Single)
Difficulty: Medium (High if AoE)

2. Talent Tree & Leveling Order

  • Level 10–19: Improved Frostbolt (5/5), Frostbite (3/3), Ice Shards (2/5)
  • Level 20–29: Ice Shards (5/5), Cold Snap (1/1), Piercing Ice (3/3), Shatter (1/5)
  • Level 30–39: Shatter (5/5), Ice Block (1/1), Cone of Cold (1/1), Ice Barrier (1/1)
    Ice Barrier makes you incredibly durable against melee attacks.

3. Rotation & Playstyle

Single Target

Keep Ice Barrier up. Cast Frostbolt from max range. If they get close, Frost Nova, take distance, and resume Frostbolt.

AoE Grinding (Level 22+)

Gather melee mobs → Frost Nova → Blink away → Blizzard. Cone of Cold to slow if Blizzard breaks. Very fast leveling if mastered.

4. Stat Priority

Intellect > Stamina > Spirit

Warlock Guide

Level 1-60

1. Quick Facts

Best Race (PvE): Gnome / Orc
Best Race (PvP): Gnome / Undead
Leveling Spec: Affliction (Drain Tanking)
Difficulty: Easy (Infinite Mana)

2. Talent Tree & Leveling Order

  • Level 10–19: Improved Corruption (5/5), Suppression (4/5)
  • Level 20–29: Nightfall (2/2), Grim Reach (2/2), Siphon Life (1/1)
  • Level 30–39: Shadow Mastery (5/5), Dark Pact (1/1)
    Dark Pact lets you drain mana from your pet, eliminating downtime forever.

3. Rotation & Playstyle

Single Target

Send Voidwalker (1-39) or Succubus (40+). Cast Siphon Life → Curse of Agony → Corruption. Life Tap for mana, Drain Life to heal it back.

Multi-Target

DoT up 2-3 targets at once. Fear one, let the Voidwalker tank the second, and drain tank the third yourself.

4. Stat Priority

Stamina > Intellect > Spirit

Druid Guide

Level 1-60

1. Quick Facts

Best Race (PvE): Night Elf / Tauren
Best Race (PvP): Night Elf / Tauren
Leveling Spec: Feral Combat
Difficulty: Medium (Very High Mobility)

2. Talent Tree & Leveling Order

  • Level 10–19: Ferocity (5/5), Feral Instinct (5/5)
  • Level 20–29: Feline Swiftness (2/2), Feral Charge (1/1), Sharpened Claws (3/3), Blood Frenzy (2/2)
  • Level 30–39: Faerie Fire Feral (1/1), Heart of the Wild (5/5), Leader of the Pack (1/1)
    Cat form is your main leveling tool from level 20 to 60.

3. Rotation & Playstyle

Single Target

Stealth (Prowl) → Ravage (opener) → Claw to 5 combo points → Ferocious Bite. Keep Faerie Fire active.

Multi-Target

Switch to Bear Form. Use Swipe for AoE damage and Demoralizing Roar to reduce incoming damage. Shift out to heal if necessary.

4. Stat Priority

Agility > Strength > Stamina > Intellect

Weapon DPS does not matter for Feral forms! Only the raw stats (Agility/Strength) on the weapon count towards your damage.

▶ Cozy Sleeping Bag Questline

The Student Fodder and Cozy Sleeping Bag give you a massive stacking XP buff. Here is how to unlock it step-by-step:

  1. Start the Quest: Horde starts at the burnt out camp south of Camp Taurajo in The Barrens. Alliance starts in Westfall near the Alexston Farm dirt mound.
  2. Stonetalon / Loch Modan: Follow the note to the Sun Rock Retreat path (Horde) or the Stonewrought Dam (Alliance). Click the interactable item on the ground.
  3. Hillsbrad Foothills: Head to the secret camp on the mountain ridge separating Hillsbrad and Alterac Mountains.
  4. Wailing Caverns (Final Step): Climb the rocks directly above the Wailing Caverns entrance in the Barrens. Complete a small jumping puzzle to find the messenger's camp and loot the bag!

▶ Legacy System (Account-Wide)

WoW Forever features a modernized Legacy System, ensuring your hard work is respected across all your characters (Alts).

Reputation Sharing

Unlock a reputation once, and the progress is shared. You only need to grind Argent Dawn or Timbermaw Hold on your main.

Warbank Access

A new tab in the bank allows you to deposit crafting materials, gold, and bind-on-account items instantly accessible by any alt.

Transmog Unlocks

Looting any soulbound item permanently unlocks its appearance for your entire account, regardless of armor type.

▶ Essential Addons

You cannot play efficiently without these core addons installed in your _classic_/Interface/AddOns folder:

Questie

Absolute necessity. Shows available quests and objectives on your map. Pro-Tip: Go to settings and enable "Show low level quests" if you are out-leveling zones but still need the reputation.

Bagnon

Merges all your separate bags into one massive window. Also lets you see the inventory and gold of your offline alts when hovering over items.

Details!

The most accurate damage meter. Use this to track not just your DPS, but your threat generation (TinyThreat plugin) and interrupt tracking in dungeons.

Horde Leveling Routes

Select your starting race to view the optimized 1-60 route:

Orc & Troll Route

Level 1-12: Durotar

Clear Valley of Trials. Move to Sen'jin Village at level 5. Do Echo Isles, then Razor Hill. Finish Tiragarde Keep before going to Orgrimmar and taking the zeppelin/running to Barrens.

Level 12-25: The Barrens

Hub out of Crossroads. Do all Oasis quests. Around level 15, head south to Camp Taurajo. Do Wailing Caverns at level 18+. Grind Harpies and complete Ishamuhale before leaving.

Level 25-60: Shared Horde Route

Stonetalon Mountains & Ashenvale (25-30). Stranglethorn Vale & Arathi (30-40). Tanaris & Feralas (40-50). Un'Goro, Plaguelands, Winterspring (50-60).

Tauren Route

Level 1-10: Mulgore

Start in Camp Narache. Grind heavily on the way to Bloodhoof Village. Complete the well quests and venture to Thunder Bluff. The mob density is great here.

Level 10-25: The Barrens

Run east into the Barrens. Stop at Camp Taurajo to grab flight path, then run to Crossroads. Merge into the main Barrens route (Oasis, Wailing Caverns, Harpies).

Level 25-60: Shared Horde Route

Stonetalon Mountains (very close by!) & Ashenvale (25-30). Stranglethorn Vale & Arathi (30-40). Tanaris & Feralas (40-50). Un'Goro, Plaguelands, Winterspring (50-60).

Undead Route

Level 1-12: Tirisfal Glades

Deathknell into Brill. Complete Agamand Mills and the Scarlet Crusade tower quests. Excellent quest flow and close hubs.

Level 12-20: Silverpine vs. The Barrens

Silverpine Forest is decent until level 15, but then it becomes very difficult. Pro-tip: Take the zeppelin from Undercity to Orgrimmar at level 13-14 and do The Barrens instead for much faster XP.

Level 20-60: Shared Horde Route

If you stayed in Silverpine, do Shadowfang Keep. Otherwise, follow the main route: Ashenvale (25-30), Stranglethorn (30-40), Tanaris (40-50), Un'Goro & Plaguelands (50-60).

Alliance Leveling Routes

Select your starting race to view the optimized 1-60 route:

Human Route

Level 1-10: Elwynn Forest

Northshire Abbey -> Goldshire. Group up Fargodeep Mine and Gold Dust quests. Push east to Eastvale Logging camp for Princess and Red Linen Goods.

Level 10-20: Westfall & Redridge

Go to Westfall, do the Defias Brotherhood chain. At level 15, bounce to Redridge Mountains for a few quests to bridge the level gap, then return to Westfall for Deadmines at level 18+.

Level 20-60: Shared Alliance Route

Duskwood (20-30 - fantastic zone!). Stranglethorn Vale & Southshore (30-40). Feralas & Tanaris (40-50). Searing Gorge, Un'goro, Plaguelands (50-60).

Dwarf & Gnome Route

Level 1-10: Dun Morogh

Coldridge Valley into Kharanos. Grind troggs and wendigos. Protect the quarry and do the Gnomeregan pre-quests.

Level 10-20: Loch Modan & Darkshore

Loch Modan is great until level 14. After that, take the tram to Stormwind, boat to Darkshore, and do quests there from 14-18. It's much faster than staying in Loch Modan.

Level 20-60: Shared Alliance Route

Merge into Wetlands & Duskwood (20-30). Stranglethorn Vale & Southshore (30-40). Feralas & Tanaris (40-50). Searing Gorge, Un'goro, Plaguelands (50-60).

Night Elf Route

Level 1-10: Teldrassil

Shadowglen into Dolanaar. Do the Ban'ethil Barrow Den quests carefully (easy to get lost/die). Hit level 10 and go to Darnassus.

Level 10-20: Darkshore

Auberdine is an amazing hub. Do all the Beached Sea Creature quests along the coast. Group up for the Tower of Althalaxx. Run Deadmines (via boat to Wetlands, run to Ironforge, tram to Stormwind) or Wailing Caverns at level 18+.

Level 20-60: Shared Alliance Route

Ashenvale & Wetlands (20-30). Stranglethorn Vale & Southshore (30-40). Feralas & Tanaris (40-50). Searing Gorge, Un'goro, Plaguelands (50-60).

Class Professions Guide

Choosing the right professions while leveling can either save you gold, provide powerful Bind-on-Pickup gear, or give you crucial PvP utility. Here are the optimal combinations for every class.

Warrior

Engineering & Mining

Why: Essential for both PvE and PvP. Iron Grenades provide an AoE stun and extra damage, while Target Dummies can save you from a bad pull. Mining fuels the expensive engineering mats.

Paladin

Engineering & Mining

Why: Paladins lack gap closers and ranged pulls. Engineering gives you bombs to pull from afar and stun runners. It's the ultimate utility boost for leveling.

Hunter

Skinning & Leatherworking

Why: Hunters kill beasts very quickly. Skinning generates raw gold, and Leatherworking allows you to craft your own Agility gear (and eventually Devilsaur Armor at 60).

Rogue

Skinning & Leatherworking

Why: Very similar to Hunters. You can craft your own leather gear. Alternatively, pick Herbalism & Alchemy to make your own Blinding Powder (Swiftthistle) and healing potions.

Priest

Tailoring & Enchanting

Why: Tailoring provides bags and the powerful Bind-on-Pickup Truefaith Vestments at 60. Enchanting lets you craft powerful wands early on (Greater Magic Wand) for smooth leveling.

Shaman

Herbalism & Alchemy

Why: Ghost Wolf allows you to travel fast between herb nodes. Alchemy provides constant mana potions, which are incredibly important to keep your leveling speed high.

Mage

Tailoring & Herbalism

Why: Tailoring for the Robe of the Archmage. Herbalism is massive money for Mages because you can easily AoE farm dungeons like Zul'Farrak and gather rare herbs safely.

Warlock

Tailoring & Enchanting

Why: Tailoring allows you to craft the incredible Robes of the Void at 60. Disenchanting the green items you get while drain-tanking yields steady passive income.

Druid

Herbalism & Alchemy

Why: Tauren Druids gather herbs extremely fast. Travel form makes node-hopping a breeze. Alchemy provides consumables that make leveling in Bear/Cat form even more efficient.
