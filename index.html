Warrior Guide

Level 1-60

1. Quick Facts

Best Race (PvE): Human (Ally) / Orc (Horde)
Best Race (PvP): Gnome (Ally) / Orc (Horde)
Leveling Spec: Arms
Difficulty: Hard (Gear Dependent)
Professions: Mining / Engineering

2. Talent Tree & Leveling Order

  • Level 10–19: Cruelty (5/5), Deflection (4/5)
    Focus on getting flat crit chance first for consistent rage generation.
  • Level 20–29: Tactical Mastery (5/5), Improved Overpower (2/2), Deep Wounds (3/3)
    Tactical Mastery is mandatory for smooth stance dancing.
  • Level 30–39: Impale (2/2), Sweeping Strikes (1/1), Weapon Mastery (2/2), Mortal Strike (1/1)
    Sweeping Strikes and Mortal Strike are your biggest leveling power spikes.

3. Rotation & Playstyle

Single Target

Charge in → Rend → Mortal Strike (on cooldown) → Overpower (when it procs) → Sunder Armor (if target lives long enough). Use Heroic Strike only if you have excess rage (over 60).

Multi-Target & Survival

Pop Sweeping Strikes, switch to Berserker Stance, use Whirlwind, and Cleave. Hamstring kiting is crucial to survive against hard-hitting mobs. Save Bloodrage for emergency rage.

4. Stat Priority

Weapon DPS > Strength > Agility > Stamina

Always prioritize upgrading your weapon. A higher DPS weapon will increase your leveling speed significantly more than any single piece of armor.
